Orthodox Prayer Book

01 How to pray at home

Personal prayer extends the prayer of the Church. The Fathers advise having a rule: a few prayers said every day at a set time, even brief ones, rather than long prayers now and then.

  • Before the icons. One prays preferably standing, facing the icons, before a lit lamp.
  • Without haste. A few prayers said attentively are better than a long text recited absent-mindedly.
  • A rule within your strength. This page offers a short rule. It can be lengthened or shortened with the advice of your confessor.
  • On busy days, St Seraphim’s rule is enough: the Lord’s Prayer three times, “O Theotokos and Virgin” three times and the Creed once.

The Lord’s Prayer

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.

Хлеб наш насу́щный даждь нам днесь; и оста́ви нам до́лги на́ша, я́коже и мы оставля́ем должнико́м на́шим; и не введи́ нас во искуше́ние, но изба́ви нас от лука́ваго.

03 Morning prayers

After the opening prayers:

Troparion to the Holy Trinity

Having risen from sleep, we fall down before thee, O Good One, and sing to thee, O Mighty One, the hymn of the angels: Holy, holy, holy art thou, O God; through the Theotokos have mercy on us.

Church Slavonic text

Воста́вше от сна, припа́даем Ти, Бла́же, и а́нгельскую песнь вопие́м Ти, Си́льне: Свят, Свят, Свят еси́, Бо́же, Богоро́дицею поми́луй нас.

The Creed

I believe in one God, the Father Almighty, Maker of heaven and earth, and of all things visible and invisible. And in one Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, the Only-begotten, begotten of the Father before all ages; Light of Light, true God of true God, begotten, not made, of one essence with the Father, by whom all things were made; who for us men and for our salvation came down from heaven, and was incarnate of the Holy Spirit and the Virgin Mary, and became man; and was crucified for us under Pontius Pilate, and suffered, and was buried; and rose again on the third day according to the Scriptures; and ascended into heaven, and sitteth at the right hand of the Father; and shall come again with glory to judge the living and the dead, whose kingdom shall have no end. And in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the Giver of life, who proceedeth from the Father, who with the Father and the Son together is worshipped and glorified, who spake by the prophets. In one Holy, Catholic and Apostolic Church. I confess one baptism for the remission of sins. I look for the resurrection of the dead, and the life of the age to come. Amen.

Church Slavonic text

И во еди́наго Го́спода Иису́са Христа́, Сы́на Бо́жия, Единоро́днаго, И́же от Отца́ рожде́ннаго пре́жде всех век; Све́та от Све́та, Бо́га и́стинна от Бо́га и́стинна, рожде́нна, несотворе́нна, единосу́щна Отцу́, И́мже вся бы́ша. Нас ра́ди челове́к и на́шего ра́ди спасе́ния сше́дшаго с небе́с и воплоти́вшагося от Ду́ха Свя́та и Мари́и Де́вы и вочелове́чшася. Распя́таго же за ны при Понти́йстем Пила́те, и страда́вша, и погребе́нна. И воскре́сшаго в тре́тий день по Писа́нием. И возше́дшаго на небеса́, и седя́ща одесну́ю Отца́. И па́ки гряду́щаго со сла́вою суди́ти живы́м и ме́ртвым, Его́же Ца́рствию не бу́дет конца́. Во еди́ну Святу́ю, Собо́рную и Апо́стольскую Це́рковь. Испове́дую еди́но креще́ние во оставле́ние грехо́в. Ча́ю воскресе́ния ме́ртвых, и жи́зни бу́дущаго ве́ка. Ами́нь.

First morning prayer, of St Macarius the Great

O God, cleanse me a sinner, for I have never done anything good in thy sight; but deliver me from the evil one, and let thy will be in me, that I may open my unworthy lips without condemnation and praise thy holy name, of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Church Slavonic text

Бо́же, очи́сти мя гре́шнаго, я́ко николи́же сотвори́х благо́е пред Тобо́ю; но изба́ви мя от лука́ваго, и да бу́дет во мне во́ля Твоя́, да неосужде́нно отве́рзу уста́ моя недосто́йная и восхвалю́ и́мя Твое́ свято́е, Отца́ и Сы́на и Свята́го Ду́ха, ны́не и при́сно и во ве́ки веко́в. Ами́нь.

Prayer to the guardian angel

O holy angel, who standest by my wretched soul and my passionate life, forsake me not a sinner, nor depart from me because of my intemperance. Give no place to the evil demon to master me through the violence of this mortal body; strengthen my poor and feeble hand, and guide me on the way of salvation. Yea, O holy angel of God, guardian and protector of my wretched soul and body, forgive me all wherewith I have offended thee all the days of my life, and if I have sinned in any way this past night, shelter me this day, and keep me from every temptation of the enemy, that I may not anger God by any sin; and pray to the Lord for me, that he may establish me in his fear and show me worthy, his servant, of his goodness. Amen.

Church Slavonic text

04 Evening prayers

After the opening prayers:

Prayer for the forgiveness of the day

O Lord our God, as thou art good and the lover of mankind, forgive me wherein I have sinned this day in word, deed and thought. Grant me peaceful and undisturbed sleep. Send thy guardian angel to shelter and keep me from all evil, for thou art the guardian of our souls and bodies, and to thee we send up glory, to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Church Slavonic text

Го́споди Бо́же наш, е́же согреши́х во дни сем сло́вом, де́лом и помышле́нием, я́ко Благ и Человеколю́бец прости́ ми. Ми́рен сон и безмяте́жен да́руй ми. А́нгела Твоего́ храни́теля посли́, покрыва́юща и соблюда́юща мя от вся́каго зла, я́ко Ты еси́ храни́тель душа́м и телесе́м на́шим, и Тебе́ сла́ву возсыла́ем, Отцу́ и Сы́ну и Свято́му Ду́ху, ны́не и при́сно и во ве́ки веко́в. Ами́нь.

Evening prayer to the guardian angel

O angel of Christ, my holy guardian and protector of my soul and body, forgive me all wherein I have sinned this day, and deliver me from every wile of the enemy, that I may not anger my God by any sin; but pray for me, a sinful and unworthy servant, that thou mayest show me worthy of the goodness and mercy of the All-holy Trinity, and of the Mother of my Lord Jesus Christ, and of all the saints. Amen.

Church Slavonic text

05 Before and after meals

As a family, pray standing, facing the icons; the eldest or the head of the family may say the prayer aloud.

Before meals

The Lord’s Prayer may be said, then:

The eyes of all look to thee with hope, O Lord, and thou givest them their food in due season; thou openest thy bountiful hand and fillest every living thing with thy favour.

Church Slavonic text

О́чи все́х на Тя́, Го́споди, упова́ют, и Ты́ дае́ши и́м пи́щу во благовре́мении, отверза́еши Ты́ ще́друю ру́ку Твою́ и исполня́еши вся́кое живо́тно благоволе́ния.

Then bless the food and drink with the sign of the cross.

O Lord Jesus Christ our God, bless our food and drink, through the prayers of thy most pure Mother and of all thy saints, for thou art blessed unto ages of ages. Amen.

Church Slavonic text

Го́споди, Иису́се Христе́, Бо́же на́ш, благослови́ на́м пи́щу и питие́ моли́твами Пречи́стыя Твоея́ Ма́тере и все́х святы́х Твои́х, я́ко благослове́н во ве́ки веко́в. Ами́нь.

After meals

Church Slavonic text
