26 November 2026

Today is the feast of Saint John Chrysostom

On 26 November (13 November in the Julian calendar), the Church commemorates Saint John Chrysostom, Archbishop of Constantinople, who died in exile in 407 saying: “Glory to God for all things!” His preaching earned him the name “Golden Mouth”.

It is his Liturgy that we celebrate almost every Sunday, and his homily that we read on Paschal night. Discover his life and words in a new article.

The church reopens on 6 December, the feast of St Alexander Nevsky

We are delighted to announce the reopening of our church. The first Divine Liturgy will be celebrated on Sunday 6 December at 10:30, the feast of St Alexander Nevsky, patron saint of our church.

22 November 2026

The Nativity Fast begins on 28 November

From 28 November to 6 January, the Church invites us to prepare for the feast of the Nativity of Christ, which we celebrate on 7 January, with forty days of fasting, prayer and generosity.

To help you, three new articles are online: the Nativity Fast, Orthodox Christmas and a guide to Orthodox fasting.
