II · Lives of the saints

Four Russian saints

1221–1263 · Our patron saint

Saint Alexander Nevsky

“God is not in might, but in truth.”

Prince of Novgorod, defender of Russia, who died a monk with the name Alexis: the patron of our church.

Read → 1754–1833

Saint Seraphim of Sarov

“The true aim of our Christian life is the acquisition of the Holy Spirit of God.”

The hermit of the forest, the elder who greeted everyone with “My joy, Christ is risen!”, and his rule of prayer for lay people.

Lire → 1829–1908

Saint John of Kronstadt

“Prayer is the breath of the soul.”

The priest of Kronstadt, friend of the poor, whom all Russia came to consult, author of My Life in Christ.

Read → 1866–1938

Saint Silouan the Athonite

“Keep your mind in hell, and despair not.”

A Russian peasant who became a monk on Mount Athos, witness of God’s love, of love for enemies and of prayer for the whole world.

  3. The lives of the saints, to see this prayer lived.
  4. The Philokalia, to go deeper with the Fathers.
Advice

How to read

The Fathers recommend reading slowly, prayerfully and with guidance.

A little, every dayA few lines read slowly and re-read are worth more than long readings that bear no fruit.
With prayerBegin with a short prayer and end by thanking God for what you have received.
Within the ChurchReading does not replace the Liturgy or the sacraments: it carries them into everyday life.
With guidanceTalk to your confessor about your reading; he can advise you on what suits you.
