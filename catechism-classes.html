Catechism at the parish

01 Who is it for?

  • Children and teenagers: discovering Christ, the feasts of the year, prayers and the life of the Church, at their own pace.
  • Adults who want to know the Orthodox faith better, whether they have always been Orthodox or are just discovering it.
  • Catechumens, that is, adults preparing for baptism or to be received into the Orthodox Church.
  • Parents and godparents preparing for a baptism who wish to understand their commitment.

02 What is taught

Catechism is not first of all a lesson but an initiation into the life of the Church: one learns the faith in order to pray better, take part in the Liturgy and live as a Christian.

  • The Creed: God the Trinity, Christ, the Church.
  • Prayer, personal and common, the sign of the cross, icons.
  • The Divine Liturgy and the sacraments.
  • Holy Scripture and the lives of the saints.
  • The liturgical year: the feasts and fasts, which you can follow in the parish calendar.

The online Orthodox catechism, with its 313 questions and answers, supports this journey.

03 Signing up

Groups, days and the language of the meetings are arranged with the priest according to who signs up. To take part, write to us through the contact form, stating:

  • who it is for (child, teenager, adult) and the children’s ages;
  • the language you would like (French or Russian);
  • when you are available.

The priest will get back to you and suggest a suitable rhythm.

In short

  • For children, teenagers, adults and catechumens.
  • The faith, prayer, the Liturgy, Scripture and the liturgical year.
  • Sign up through the contact form.
  • The online catechism to keep going at home.
